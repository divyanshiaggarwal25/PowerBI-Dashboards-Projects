# 📊 Power BI Analytics: E-Commerce & Retail Dashboards

This repository contains Power BI dashboards analyzing key operational metrics, revenue command centres, customer journey funnels, pricing elasticity, and retail sales performance.

---

## 🛍️ Project 1: E-Commerce Data Analytics
An end-to-end e-commerce analysis covering revenue dynamics, customer behaviors, pricing strategies, and marketing performance.

### Key Reports & Insights:
1. **Executive Revenue Command Centre:** Tracks total revenue, capture rates, and performance across web/app channels and geographic regions.
2. **Customer Behavior & Engagement:** Analyzes view-to-cart conversion funnels, category engagement, and weekly traffic trends.
3. **Pricing Intelligence & Margin Optimization:** Evaluates average pricing, unit discounts, and price elasticity across product categories.
4. **Promotional Effectiveness Tracker:** Monitors promo-driven revenue, discount efficiency, and weekday vs. weekend promotional lifts.

---

## 🛒 Project 2: Retail Analytics (RetailMax)
A executive performance dashboard focused on sales trajectory, fulfillment efficiency, and customer volume.

### Key Reports & Insights:
- **RetailMax Executive Performance Dashboard:** Monitors overall revenue trends, cancellation rates, net units sold, and performance across zones and product categories.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Business Intelligence:** Power BI Desktop
- **Data Transformation & Modeling:** Power Query, DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- **Output:** Multi-page Interactive PDF Reports
